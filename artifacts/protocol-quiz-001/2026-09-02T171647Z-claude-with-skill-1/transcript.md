Written to `answer.md`. The short version:

**Build on today's Keccak hexary MPT proofs (`eth_getProof` shape), anchored to a sync-committee-verified beacon header — not Verkle, and not (yet) the binary tree.**

What I verified today (2026-09-02), rather than recalling:

- **Verkle is dead as a build target.** EIP-6800 and EIP-7612 are both `Stagnant`, with no fork relationship. The 2024-era "Verkle is next" material you'll find in blogs and even in some recent press is stale.
- **The genuine direction is the unified binary tree (EIP-7864)** — SNARK-friendly, accounts/storage/code merged under 32-byte keys. But it is `Draft` spec maturity with **no fork relationship at all**: absent from Glamsterdam's meta EIP, and absent from Hegotá's meta EIP *including its 52-entry "Proposed for Inclusion" list*. Its own hash function is still open (BLAKE3 is experimental; Keccak and Poseidon2 are candidates).
- **Timing.** Last mainnet fork was Fusaka (2025-12-03). Glamsterdam is SFI'd and on the public Plataberget testnet since 2026-08-20, mainnet targeted H2 2026 — and it changes nothing in the state trie. Hegotá's meta EIP is `Draft` with only FOCIL and Frame Transaction scheduled. So a state-trie change is at minimum two forks out, unscheduled, and blocked on an unresolved hash choice.

So: hard dependency on MPT proofs is safe; **zero** dependency on binary-tree proofs. The recommendation puts the proof backend behind a `StateProofBackend` interface with a versioned witness envelope, so the eventual migration (during which both trees coexist) is a new implementation rather than a rewrite.

Two things worth flagging that I'd watch more closely than the trie itself: **EIP-7862 (Delayed State Root)** and **EIP-7807 (SSZ execution blocks)** are both Hegotá-proposed and would break naive header→state-root assumptions well before the trie ever changes. On the positive side, **EIP-7688** is scheduled in Glamsterdam and stabilizes SSZ generalized indices — a direct win for your light-client proof paths.
