The core risk is one cluster on `/payouts`: the page uses raw wagmi `useWriteContract` instead of `useScaffoldWriteContract`. That single choice cascades into no wrong-network guard (wallet on the wrong chain still gets live Approve/Send buttons), no pre-flight simulation, resolution at wallet-signature rather than confirmation (so the form clears while the tx is still pending), and `catch { console.error }` — meaning reverts and rejections are completely silent to the user.

Other ship-blockers worth calling out:

- **USDC is hand-added to `deployedContracts.ts`** — that file is autogenerated; the next `yarn deploy` deletes the entry and balance/approve silently break. Belongs in `externalContracts.ts`.
- **Recipient is a raw text input** — no ENS, no validation, and with no simulation nothing catches a typo before an irreversible USDC transfer.
- **`fromBlock: 0n`** forces a 33.7M-block `getLogs` scan of Base on every page load; the contract's `deployedOnBlock` is already in the ABI file and is the hook's default.
- **`bg-black text-white`** on the page root bypasses DaisyUI — light-mode users get a black page with a light header, and the theme toggle appears dead.
- **RPC posture**: `rpcOverrides` is empty, and `wagmiConfig` deliberately ranks the default Alchemy key *behind* the bare public transport — combined with the above, this throttles on day one.

Three things I couldn't verify from here and listed as open questions: whether `NEXT_PUBLIC_ALCHEMY_API_KEY` is actually set on the production host (it's only in `.env.example`), whether the deployed contract is verified on Basescan, and whether exact-amount vs unlimited approve is the intended UX.
