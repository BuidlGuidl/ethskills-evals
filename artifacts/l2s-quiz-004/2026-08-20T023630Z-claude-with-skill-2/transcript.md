**Pipeline deltas** (nine, several recurring, not one-time): pinned dual toolchain; `cargo stylus check` pre-flight; deploy is *two* operations — code + **activation** via the `ArbWasm` precompile at `0x…0071`, so make CI idempotent; a hard compressed-size gate; a **CacheManager bid** with eviction alarms; **reactivation monitoring** tied to paging, since a silently deactivated scorer takes your dispute path offline; published source→codehash verification; a Nitro devnode instead of anvil; and the step that actually earns the one-implementation claim — a differential harness running native/firmware/wasm32 over the same vectors, because `usize` is 32-bit on wasm and overflow-checks differ by profile.

**Two things worth your attention before you build:** I'd classify six months of dispute tickets first — if operators are arguing about spoofing, dropped samples, or coverage attribution rather than the arithmetic, this adjudicates the part nobody contested. And I assumed your ERC-20/staking are on Arbitrum or are movable; the Solidity↔Stylus call only works same-chain, so if they hold real value elsewhere, the answer flips to a RISC Zero/SP1 coprocessor (same crate, verifies anywhere). That's the one input that could change the recommendation outright.
