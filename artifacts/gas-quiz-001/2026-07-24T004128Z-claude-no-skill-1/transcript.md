**Arbitrum One**, with Base as the alternative if your users are Coinbase-native.

The core argument is that gas shouldn't decide this. A full escrow lifecycle (~220k gas) costs about $0.03 on Base and $0.08 on Arbitrum — a five-cent spread, or 0.0025% of your smallest $2,000 job. Meanwhile you'd be holding real float: at 200 concurrent jobs averaging $10k, that's $2M in the contract. Annual gas on that is ~$80; a 1% chance of a chain-level freeze costs $20,000 in expectation. So I ranked on custody safety first.

All three major L2s are Stage 1 in 2026, but Arbitrum is reported as the only production optimistic rollup with fully permissionless fraud proofs — anyone can challenge a bad state root without trusting the operator. That plus the 7-day upgrade notice is what you want under money you don't own.

Ruled out: L1 (median is 7¢ today at 0.162 gwei, but the tail hits $40.70 at 100 gwei and you can't quote an unpredictable fee), Polygon PoS (sidechain security for your float, no compensating benefit), Solana (worth reconsidering only if your freelancers are already there).

Two caveats worth your attention:
- The 220,000 gas figure is my estimate from typical ERC-20 escrow patterns. Measure it against your actual contract.
