Two consistent shifts:

1. **Source chain.** Baseline runs reason from the EIPs repo and EthMagicians. Skill runs go to forkcast first, as the skill's "How to Actually Check" section prescribes (0/6 baseline runs used forkcast meaningfully vs 5/6 skill runs).
2. **Vocabulary of commitment.** Skill runs answer in SFI/CFI/DFI terms — status against a named fork — rather than prose hedging. On the goal task that is a 20× jump in usage.

Both are real behavioral changes. Neither was needed to pass, because the expects grade the *conclusion*, and the model reaches the right conclusion from memory.

**Trigger rate is unverified.** `claude -p` emits only the final message, so the saved transcripts do not show tool calls; I cannot report a Skill-tool trigger rate the way the frontend-playbook eval did. The forkcast/SFI signature above is a proxy — it implies the skill was read in at least 5 of 6 `with_skill` runs, but it is not direct evidence. Capturing `--output-format stream-json` would fix this for future evals.
