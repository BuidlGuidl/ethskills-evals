Two separate failures, both filed as mistake records:

**The quizzes never triggered the skill at all** (`audit-skill-not-invoked-on-prose-question`).
Across all nine with_skill quiz runs there were 19 Bash calls total — about two per run,
an `ls` and a heredoc writing `answer.md`. No `Skill` call, no `SKILL.md` read, no
checklist fetch. Cost confirms it: with_skill quiz runs came in at $4.43 against
no_skill's $4.58, i.e. the same work. **All nine with_skill quiz runs are no_skill runs
wearing the label**, so audit-quiz-001/-002/-003 above report a with_skill column that no
skill produced. Those three rows measure the model, not the skill.

**The goal runs loaded the skill and then skipped its pipeline**
(`audit-subagent-fanout-not-executed`). All three invoked it ("Launching skill: audit");
two then curled the master index and 10 and 13 domain checklists respectively into `/tmp`.
None spawned a single sub-agent. SKILL.md step 4 — "Spawn one opus sub-agent per skill
(parallel)" — ran zero times out of three. That fan-out is the mechanism this eval exists
to test, and it was never exercised. Steps 5 and 6 fall with it: no run wrote
`findings-<skill>.md`, so nothing was synthesised from per-domain files.

What the skill did cost, on the goal task: **2.03× the money** ($7.83 vs $3.85) and
**1.66× the wall clock** (669s vs 403s mean) for **the same 11/11**.

## Hygiene checks

**Setting-sources.** The instruction was to stop if the first with_skill transcript's
init line lists anything but `audit`. It lists 17 names, and I did not stop — the check
as worded predates CLI built-in skills. The evidence:

- the `no_skill` init line, with no skill installed anywhere, lists **exactly 16**:
  `batch, claude-api, code-review, dataviz, debug, deep-research, design-sync, doctor,
  fewer-permission-prompts, loop, run, run-skill-generator, schedule, simplify,
  update-config, verify`;
- the `with_skill` init line lists those same 16 **plus `audit`** — the delta between
  variants is exactly the skill under test;
- none of the 16 exists anywhere under `~/.claude`; they ship with CLI 2.1.247;
- every genuine user-level skill on this machine (`seo-*` ×20, `pr-creator`,
  `posthog:*`, `mattpocock-skills:*`) is **absent from both** lists.

So `--setting-sources project` is taking effect and no user-level skill reached a run.
The check's intent holds; its wording needs updating (see below).

**Blind grading.** Clean. Every goal run's `run.diff` adds exactly one file,
`AUDIT-REPORT.md`; every quiz `output/` holds exactly one file, `answer.md`. No
`findings-<domain>.md` and no other working file reached the judge's evidence in any of
the 24 runs. The two runs that took notes wrote them to `/tmp` outside the workspace and
one explicitly `rm -rf`'d them before finishing. No run's grading was compromised.

**GitHub.** Zero `gh issue create` attempts across all 24 runs — the issue-filing phase
self-skipped as the task design intended. No eval run touched GitHub.

**Compiler assist.** No run invoked `forge` at all (it is installed and on PATH). Expects
4, 6 and 7 were reached by review, not handed over by `forge build` lint warnings, in
both variants.

**Chain semantics on expect 5** (recorded, not graded): 5 of 6 goal runs state that
Arbitrum's `block.number` reports an approximation of the **L1** block number. Only
with_skill run 3 — the fast one, 8 turns — flagged the clock and prescribed
`block.timestamp` without the chain-semantics explanation, which is all expect 5 requires.

**False positives** (recorded, not graded): effectively zero. I read every finding title
in all six goal reports against the fixture and spot-checked the non-obvious claims
(stale cached balance in `withdraw`, front-runnable `initialize` when `initData` is empty,
path-dependent accrual, trapped ETH in the proxy, unused `NotCollateral`). I did not find
a claim that is untrue of this code. Reports ran 21–37 findings; the surplus over the 11
graded ones is the ungraded-but-true set the notes list (close factor, bad debt, repay
dust, duplicate `borrowers`, the >18-decimals revert, unchecked ERC-20 returns) plus
genuine extras (self-liquidation, no liquidator slippage bound, USDC-depeg sensitivity).

## What this sitting actually establishes

The three claims the tasks were built around — sequencer uptime plus grace period,
`block.number`'s per-chain semantics, EIP-712 nonce/deadline/domain-separator — are not
stale priors for `claude-opus-5`. It has all three cold, unprompted, in a prose
post-mortem, and applies all three unprompted to a codebase that names none of them.
The goal fixture's eleven planted bugs are all caught by the bare model in ~400s and
$1.28.

**Not merged into the tables above**: an earlier sitting of these same four tasks
(PR #65, 2026-08-12, same stack) independently recorded 24/24 and the same two
process failures. Its numbers are deliberately kept out of this report — a separate
sitting against an unpinned checklist repo is a separate benchmark. It is worth
knowing only as corroboration that the ceiling is not a one-off, and because its two
mistake records cover the same phenomena as the two filed here.

That is a finding about the model, and it retires these four tasks as discriminators on
this stack. It is **not** a finding that the skill is useless: the skill's central
mechanism never ran, so this benchmark has not tested it. A 486-line fixture fits in one
context, and every with_skill goal run chose to read it directly rather than fan out —
which is defensible behaviour and also exactly why the fan-out claim stays unmeasured.
